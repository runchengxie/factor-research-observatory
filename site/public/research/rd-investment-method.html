R&D investment relative to valuation

Public method note · aggregate evidence only · updated 2026-10-01
公开方法说明 · 仅聚合证据 · 更新于 2026-10-01

Research question / 研究问题

This exploratory study asks whether R&D expense scaled by market value contains cross-sectional information about future returns, and whether it remains after exposure controls. A high ratio can reflect more R&D, a lower market value, or both.

本探索性研究考察研发费用相对市值是否包含未来收益的截面信息，以及控制常见暴露后是否仍有信息。比值升高既可能来自研发费用增加，也可能来自市值下降，或二者同时发生。

Current corrected PIT result / 当前修正 PIT 结果

The corrected announcement-event PIT replay uses horizon-specific samples with 77 valid monthly cross-sections for 20-day labels and 66 for 220-day labels. Median cross-sectional sizes are approximately 4,454 and 4,230 stocks.

修正后的公告事件 PIT 回放按期限使用不同样本：20 日标签有 77 个有效月度截面，220 日标签有 66 个；每期股票数中位数约为 4,454 和 4,230。

Point-in-time and label rules / PIT 与标签口径

  • Year-to-date income fields are converted to TTM using current YTD + prior full-year − prior-year same-period YTD; annual filings use the full-year value.
    利润表年初至今累计值按“本年累计 + 上一完整年度 − 上年同期累计”还原为 TTM；年报直接使用全年值。
  • The corrected PIT replay retains announcement-event availability and forms signals at month-end. The 20- and 220-day outcomes use subsequent trading-session prices; they omit transaction costs and do not establish executable fills.
    修正 PIT 回放保留公告事件可用日，并在月末形成信号。20 日和 220 日结果使用后续交易日价格，不含交易成本，也不能证明实际可成交。
  • The EV measure is a covered-fields proxy, not a complete enterprise-value reconstruction. The capitalized-R&D factor accumulates reconstructed quarterly flows with a five-year decay schedule.
    EV 为按可覆盖字段构造的代理，并非完整企业价值重建。资本化研发因子以还原的季度流量按五年衰减方案累计。

Evidence limits and next gate / 证据边界与后续门槛

  • The corrected replay remains exploratory. Historical financial revisions, point-in-time universe and industry dates, market impact, and actual execution are not fully verified.
    修正回放仍属探索性研究。历史财报修订、股票池和行业点时日期、市场冲击及实际成交均未完全核实。
  • A separate fixed Top-200 platform probe at 25 bp one-way modeled cost reports 12.70% annualized return, 0.57 Sharpe, and -25.75% max drawdown across 89 holding periods. This probe is not the registered decile validation or a production portfolio.
    单独的固定 Top-200 平台探针在单边模型成本 25 bp 下，89 个持有区间年化收益为 12.70%、Sharpe 为 0.57、最大回撤为 -25.75%。该探针不是预注册十分组验证，也不是生产组合。
  • The frozen October 2026–September 2027 final-OOS protocol remains blocked from performance reading. No frozen holdout metrics have been produced.
    2026 年 10 月至 2027 年 9 月冻结最终 OOS 协议仍不允许读取绩效，目前没有生成冻结留出指标。
  • An earlier public annual snapshot contains a separate 2019 retrospective reconstruction extension. It is not part of the corrected current-run summary and is not revision-safe.
    较早的公开年度快照含有独立的 2019 年回溯重建扩展；它不属于修正后的当前运行汇总，也不是修订安全证据。

Interpretation / 解读边界：Positive Rank IC is an exploratory association. It does not prove causal R&D alpha, cost-adjusted returns, or production readiness.
正 Rank IC 是探索性相关，不能证明研发 Alpha 的因果性、扣成本收益或生产就绪。
